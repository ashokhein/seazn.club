## Archive

Archiving hides a division from the console and the public site — but keeps every result. Restore it from the competition's **Settings → Archived divisions**: restoring re-checks your plan's division quota, and is refused while the competition itself is **completed or archived** ([a finished competition takes no new divisions](/help/getting-started/create-a-competition)).

Use it for: last season's divisions cluttering the console, or a division created by mistake **after** matches were played.

## Archiving and your division limit

Whether archiving hands the slot back depends on one thing — whether the division was ever really used:

- **Nothing recorded yet** — the slot comes straight back. Set a division up with the wrong sport, the wrong entrant kind or the wrong format and you can archive it and start again at no cost. Publishing a division isn't using it; only a result is.
- **Results recorded** — the slot stays with that division, archived or not. So archiving isn't a way to run more divisions than your plan allows, and last season's finished divisions still count.

A **recorded result** means a fixture that was decided, finalized or forfeited — or one abandoned but still carrying a real verdict, like a match awarded to one side or settled by a rain rule. A match abandoned with **no** result doesn't count.

So if you're at your division limit and the count looks wrong, this is usually why: an archived division that was played is still holding its place. The console says so at both ends: before you archive, and at the refusal.

## What the Danger zone tells you before you archive

On the division's **Settings tab → Danger zone**, the two cases above read differently — because they cost different things:

- **Archiving would keep the slot spent.** An amber note sits above the **Archive** button: *"This division has recorded results, so archiving it will not free a division slot."* The confirm step says the same thing rather than the usual line — the division disappears from your console and the public site, nothing is destroyed, you can restore it, but the slot does not come back.
- **Archiving is free.** No note, and the confirm step says what it always said: the division stops counting against your plan.

Nothing is blocked either way — this is a price named before the click rather than after it. Without it you could archive a played division, see fewer divisions on screen than your plan allows, and later meet a paywall with no visible cause.

If our team has [waived that division's slot](#common-questions), the warning is gone and the ordinary confirm step is back, because the archive really is free by then.

## When a limit refusal blames the archive

The same rule from the other end: you're refused a division while the console shows fewer divisions than your plan allows. Both refusals name the cause:

- the **division wizard**, when creating one is refused;
- **Settings → Archived divisions**, when restoring one is refused.

Both add the same line under the upgrade panel: *"Divisions with recorded results count toward your limit even after they are archived."*

It only appears when those archived divisions really are the reason. If the divisions you can see already fill the limit on their own, releasing an archived one would change nothing — so the line stays away rather than pointing you at something that isn't the cause. What helps then is a bigger limit, or archiving a division you can see that never recorded a result.

## Delete

Delete permanently removes a division — allowed only while it has **no recorded play**. Once results exist you'll be offered archive instead: results are a shared record (players point at them), so they never silently vanish.

An archived division can be purged permanently after 30 days in the archive — a deliberate cool-off, not a second delete button.

## Common questions

**Does archiving free a Community slot?** Only if that division never recorded a result. Once a result exists the slot stays with it for good, so archiving divisions won't get you back under quota after a downgrade — completing or archiving whole **competitions** is the lever there ([what downgrading freezes](/help/billing/downgrade)).

**A slot got used up by accident — am I stuck with it?** No. Our team can waive a slot that was genuinely burned by mistake; get in touch with the organisation and the division in question. It's a support request rather than a setting. Archive the division first — a waiver releases the slot an *archived* division is still holding. Once it's done that division stops counting straight away: the slot is back in your count, the Danger zone stops warning about it, and the refusal notes above stop naming it.

**Where do I find these?** The division's **Settings tab → Danger zone**.

**Who can archive or delete?** Owners and admins; both actions require typing the division's name to confirm and land in the org audit trail.
