## Archive

Archiving hides a division from the console and the public site — but keeps every result. Restore it any time (restoring re-checks your plan's division quota).

Use it for: last season's divisions cluttering the console, or a division created by mistake **after** matches were played.

## Archiving and your division limit

Whether archiving hands the slot back depends on one thing — whether the division was ever really used:

- **Nothing recorded yet** — the slot comes straight back. Set a division up with the wrong sport, the wrong entrant kind or the wrong format and you can archive it and start again at no cost. Publishing a division isn't using it; only a result is.
- **Results recorded** — the slot stays with that division, archived or not. So archiving isn't a way to run more divisions than your plan allows, and last season's finished divisions still count.

A **recorded result** means a fixture that was decided, finalized or forfeited — or one abandoned but still carrying a real verdict, like a match awarded to one side or settled by a rain rule. A match abandoned with **no** result doesn't count.

So if you're at your division limit and the count looks wrong, this is usually why: an archived division that was played is still holding its place.

## Delete

Delete permanently removes a division — allowed only while it has **no recorded play**. Once results exist you'll be offered archive instead: results are a shared record (players point at them), so they never silently vanish.

An archived division can be purged permanently after 30 days in the archive — a deliberate cool-off, not a second delete button.

## Common questions

**Does archiving free a Community slot?** Only if that division never recorded a result. Once a result exists the slot stays with it for good, so archiving divisions won't get you back under quota after a downgrade — completing or archiving whole **competitions** is the lever there ([what downgrading freezes](/help/billing/downgrade)).

**A slot got used up by accident — am I stuck with it?** No. Our team can waive a slot that was genuinely burned by mistake; get in touch with the organisation and the division in question. It's a support request rather than a setting.

**Where do I find these?** The division's **Settings tab → Danger zone**.

**Who can archive or delete?** Owners and admins; both actions require typing the division's name to confirm and land in the org audit trail.
